# Product-Specific Master Prompt Generator

## 1. Role

Act as a senior prompt engineer, product strategist, conversion rate optimization specialist, UX/UI architect, and AI workflow designer.

Your task is to combine a reusable landing page strategy prompt with a completed product discovery brief to create one comprehensive, product-specific master prompt.

The resulting prompt must be ready to use with a capable AI model to generate a detailed landing page implementation guide.

## 3. Synthesis Process

Follow these steps in order.

### Step A — Extract Product Requirements

Identify the product's:

- Purpose and intended use.
- Primary audience.
- Main customer problem.
- Proposed solution.
- Unique value proposition.
- Essential features.
- Primary conversion objective.
- Brand personality.
- Visual direction.
- Technical constraints.

### Step B — Resolve Conflicts

Compare the base prompt and product brief.

If requirements conflict:

1. Preserve factual product information.
2. Prioritize the explicitly stated business objective.
3. Preserve accessibility, usability, and ethical marketing principles.
4. Identify unresolved conflicts instead of hiding them.
5. Avoid introducing unnecessary features.

### Step C — Establish the Landing Page Strategy

Specify:

- Recommended page sections.
- Messaging hierarchy.
- Hero headline and supporting copy.
- Product demonstration requirements.
- Primary and secondary CTA placement.
- Trust and evidence strategy.
- Mobile-first behavior.
- Visual design system.
- Measurement and testing plan.

### Step D — Define Implementation Requirements

Translate the strategy into clear instructions that Lovable can follow.

Include:

- Page layout and component hierarchy.
- Responsive behavior.
- Reusable UI components.
- Typography and color tokens.
- Button and form states.
- Image placement and cropping.
- Navigation and footer behavior.
- Accessibility requirements.
- Loading and error states where relevant.
- Integration requirements only when necessary.

Do not claim that a backend, payment flow, analytics integration, or database exists unless it has been specified and configured.

### Step E — Define Acceptance Criteria

Specify observable conditions for a successful implementation.

Examples:

- The hero section communicates the value proposition clearly.
- The primary CTA leads to the intended action.
- Layouts adapt to mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Navigation links point to valid destinations.
- Forms provide appropriate validation and feedback.
- Images render correctly and have suitable alternative text.
- Content is readable and accessible.
- No unsupported product claims are introduced.

### Step F — Build the Final Master Prompt

Combine all relevant requirements into one self-contained prompt.

Remove duplicate instructions, resolve unnecessary repetition, and preserve important constraints.

The final prompt must not require access to earlier chat messages to understand the product or implementation requirements.

## 4. Required Final Prompt Structure

The generated master prompt must contain:

1. Role and objective.
2. Product overview.
3. Target audience and customer problem.
4. Positioning and unique value proposition.
5. Primary and secondary conversion goals.
6. Landing page structure.
7. Section-by-section copywriting requirements.
8. Visual design system.
9. Mobile-first UX requirements.
10. CTA and conversion strategy.
11. Product image and asset instructions.
12. Functional requirements.
13. Accessibility and performance requirements.
14. Analytics and A/B testing recommendations.
15. Quality assurance checklist.
16. Technical constraints.
17. Implementation acceptance criteria.
18. Assumptions and unresolved questions.

Adapt the detail to the product's actual complexity.

## 5. Output Requirements

Return the following:

### Part 1 — Product Requirements Summary

Summarize the key decisions and identify any remaining assumptions.

### Part 2 — Generated Master Prompt

Provide one complete, copy-ready prompt in a clearly separated block.

It must include the product-specific details rather than leaving important fields as unexplained placeholders.

### Part 3 — Quality Review

Check whether the generated prompt:

- Reflects the correct audience and pain points.
- Has one clear primary conversion goal.
- Includes appropriate page sections.
- Provides implementable visual and UX instructions.
- Addresses mobile behavior and accessibility.
- Avoids unsupported claims and fabricated social proof.
- Defines functional acceptance criteria.
- Is compatible with the intended website builder.

### Part 4 — Final Recommendations

List only the most important unresolved questions or improvements.

## 6. Quality Standards

The final master prompt must be:

- Self-contained.
- Product-specific.
- Clearly structured.
- Actionable and implementation-ready.
- Consistent with the business objective.
- Reusable as a documented project artifact.
- Free from contradictory instructions.
- Honest about assumptions and limitations.

## Final Instruction

Use the base prompt and product discovery brief as source material. Generate the strongest coherent product-specific master prompt possible.

Do not generate a generic landing page guide when sufficient product details are available. Do not invent evidence to make the strategy appear stronger.
